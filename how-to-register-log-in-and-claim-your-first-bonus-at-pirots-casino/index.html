Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during the Pirots registration or login process, here are common problems and their solutions.

Likely cause: The email was sent to the wrong address or blocked by your spam filter. Solution: Check your spam folder, confirm you entered the correct email, and request a new verification link from the platform. If the issue persists, contact customer support.

Problem: Login fails repeatedly.
Likely cause: Incorrect username or password. Solution: Use the “forgot password” option to reset your credentials. If you still cannot log in, verify that your account has not been suspended for inactivity or policy violations.

Likely cause: The bonus requires a promo code or manual activation. Solution: Review the promotion terms to see if a code is needed. If you followed all steps correctly, contact customer support and provide your deposit details.
